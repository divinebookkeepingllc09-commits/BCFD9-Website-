About District No. 9

Serving Leon and the surrounding rural expanses with unwavering commitment, local bravery, and professional readiness.

100% Volunteer Force

Our department is powered completely by dedicated local volunteers who sacrifice their time, sleep, and comfort to protect neighbors and property.

Maximum Coverage Area

BCFD No. 9 proudly manages the largest geographic district coverage area across all fire departments within Butler County.

Always Ready

Equipped for structural fires, rescue operations, and rural brush hazards 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year.

Our Service Area

Coverage: Approximately 86,000 Acres

Butler County Fire District #9 covers the most geographic area of any fire district in Butler County, providing comprehensive emergency response across an expansive territory.
